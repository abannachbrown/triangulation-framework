Priority adjudications

Issue Why the decision matters
Medication use and social support Their proposed roles may differ with temporal ordering; treating them as confounders, or shared causes of exposure and outcome, by default could change the quantity estimated.
Cooling-to-heat applicability (A7) Determines the contribution cooling RCTs can make to the target heat question. Deciding what the target quantity is — an observed association or a defined intervention effect — is the kind of question the estimand specification guidance is designed to settle before synthesis.
Ambient temperature versus extreme heat (A3) Determines whether the two exposure constructs require distinct comparisons and interpretation.
Animal proxy and duration eligibility (A1–A2) Determines what animal evidence is eligible and how it could inform human outcomes.
Depression and suicidality (A9) Requires clarity on whether a line represents a causal hypothesis or a descriptive association. A correlation annotation is not a causal DAG edge.

Applying the three methods guidance elements

As the Summary of Evidence tables (SETs) become available, use them to examine what each stream informs and which assumptions require revision.

  1. Establish the baseline: record when the framework entered use, the available evidence, and which choices restate or extend the protocol.
  2. Use the worksheet: record the interpretation, disputed assumptions, revisions, and actions through the four steps.
  3. Carry the judgement forward: link the reported conclusion to the diagram, register, and evidence-summary versions used.

Planned evidence contributions

Planned contribution in the local materials Proposed role Issue to resolve in the ongoing review
Human observational studies Heat–outcome associations; causal interpretation under stated identifying assumptions Confounder roles, timing, exposure definition, and residual confounding.
Quasi-experimental studies Design-specific contrasts related to heat exposure What the design identifies and whether its quantity matches the target question.
Cooling / heat-acclimatisation RCTs Evidence about thermal interventions Whether and how the intervention informs the heat-harm question; cooling without high heat exposure is not automatically its inverse.
Qualitative studies Experiences, context, and candidate pathways How these inform the proposed structure without being treated as effect estimates.
Animal experiments Experimental heat effects on measured animal outcomes Proxy validity, eligibility, and availability. The source package records no eligible studies in iteration 1; confirm the current position.
Detailed questions for follow-up — evaluation

These are candidate observations for a pilot, not additional questions for the initial meeting.

  • Which assumptions or disagreements become more explicit, and do participants find that useful?
  • Does the contribution map change how a stream is interpreted or which conclusion is considered warranted?
  • How much preparation and meeting time is needed, and where is information duplicated?
  • Can the record be maintained at the next update, with clear ownership and traceable revisions?
  • What feedback do methodological, statistical, clinical, and lived-experience contributors give on its clarity?
Project materials and implementation details

The project materials include the completed protocol, draft DAG package v1.1, an assumption register, a provisional covariate-adjudication table, and a prefilled worksheet. The protocol records registration on 30 March 2026 (PROSPERO 1325620).

Evidence availability in those records is a dated snapshot. Current SET availability, the adoption date, meeting plans, and register ownership need confirmation with the review team. No effect estimates or meeting judgements are entered here.

The protocol makes convening a triangulation meeting conditional on sufficient evidence from at least two sources for an outcome. The register and evidence-gap record can be prepared before that condition is met.
