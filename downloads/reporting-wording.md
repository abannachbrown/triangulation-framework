### Candidate wording for different situations

| Situation | Illustrative wording |
|---|---|
| Association; causal identification not established | “X was associated with Y in [population and setting]; a causal interpretation would require [identifying assumptions].” |
| Contributions support a common interpretation | “Evidence from [streams] is consistent with [interpretation] for [relationship/query], conditional on [assumptions]. The key bias mechanisms assessed were [mechanisms].” |
| Agreement with potentially shared bias | “The findings agree, but shared bias through [mechanism] remains a possible explanation.” |
| Proxy and target outcomes differ | “[Stream] informs [proxy relationship]. Its relevance to [target relationship] depends on [proxy/applicability assumption], currently judged [status and rationale].” |
| Contributions diverge | “The findings diverge for [relationship/query]. Differences in [estimands, applicability, assumptions, or bias] remain [resolved/unresolved].” |
| Evidence or appraisal pending | “The planned contribution of [stream] cannot yet be assessed because [evidence/appraisal] is pending; no cross-stream judgement is made.” |
| Diagram or assumption revised | “At [date/update], we revised [relationship/assumption] because [evidence and rationale]. The conclusion refers to diagram [version] and register [version].” |
